Ideas tried in round 4, and why
  • Plots first. The round 3 plot stays: score against cost, one connected line for each model on a harness, one dot for each measured effort. At 1440px there are two plots in a row, and Full width opens one plot alone.
  • Zoom and pan, written by hand on inline SVG. Wheel or pinch zooms at the pointer, drag pans, Shift-drag zooms to a box, a drag on an axis stretches that axis, and a double click or Reset returns. Keys: + − 0 and the arrows. Each step redraws in screen pixels, so markers and lines keep their size and take less of the data area as you zoom in. At the full view, a wheel turn towards zoom-out scrolls the page as usual.
  • Round ticks at every zoom level. The cost scale is logarithmic. A wide view has 1-2-5 ticks for each decade; a view inside one decade changes to round linear steps. The score ticks use round 1-2-5 steps.
  • Labels are placed again on every redraw. Each label is tested against the boxes of all visible markers and of the labels already placed. Full view: a line gets a label only if one fits at an end of the line. Zoomed in: every visible line is tried at each dot, on eight sides and at two distances, then every dot gets its effort name (14 dots or fewer in view, or 3 times zoom). A label that does not fit is left out; it never covers a marker or another label.
  • A designed palette. Five harness colours: burnt orange, deep blue, magenta, cyan and a neutral ink, with a lighter set for the dark theme. There is no red, no yellow and no green, so nothing reads as a traffic light. The lightness steps were tuned on simulated protan, deutan and tritan views until every pair stayed apart. Colour names the harness; marker shape and line style name the model; every line you do not point at or select is dimmed.
  • Minimalist. One surface with no cards, one line of legend, one text size in a plot, one soft band for the frontier. A status line replaces the model card. The rules are collapsed.
  • The table is quantitative. One row for each model on a harness. Each benchmark column has one real score axis, with its two ends in the header. A row draws one dot for each measured effort, a line from the lowest score to the highest, and the two numbers below the ends. A filled dot is an effort a carried lane runs. The rank is a plain column; it is never drawn as an error bar.
  • Table and plots work together. Point at a row to mark its line in every plot. Point at a dot to mark the same dot on the other side. Click to keep the mark. Efforts in the plots filters every plot to one effort and draws the frontier at that effort.
