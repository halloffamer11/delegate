Round 5: selection, names with versions, reference models
  • Light first. The page opens in the light theme whatever the system says. One button switches to dark and back. The harness palette stays: codex blue, claude orange, kiro teal, agy plum, grok ink.
  • Select means show only. A click on a line, a dot, a table row or a model chip shows only that model on that harness, with all its efforts, and each plot fits itself to it. Shift-click adds or removes models to compare. With nothing selected, everything shows.
  • One click rule for every filter. Click: only this. Shift-click: add or remove. A click on the only one, or Clear, returns the group to all. Esc clears the models and the efforts.
  • Efforts inside a selection. When models are selected, a dot click picks its effort, so two Shift-clicks give "only high and xhigh" across the chosen models. The effort chips and the table dots do the same. The full line stays faint behind, so the shape of the curve stays visible.
  • Names with versions, from the data. Each label is the published name with its version, and the family word drops: "Fable 5.1", "Sol 5.6", "Astra 6", "Opus 5". The code holds no list of names.
  • One label per line. The model chips sit after their harness tag, and that tag is also the harness filter, so no badge repeats. In a plot each line ends in its name and a muted harness suffix: colour and text together give the harness.
  • Reference models. Models you do not carry have their own grey group, sorted by family. They show as grey dots. Pin one and it gets a dashed line, a label and a table row under "Reference". They never rank, never get a tier and are never the best lane. One switch hides all of them.
  • Touch. A two-finger pinch now zooms and pans on a touch screen, next to box zoom and pinch or ⌘/Ctrl-scroll on a trackpad.

Effort range

How to read this page
Line
One model on one harness, its measured efforts joined from low to max. A second harness for the same model draws only the efforts it has lanes for, with a dashed line.
Label
The model name with its version, then the harness in grey. Colour is the harness and marker shape is the model.
Marker
Filled: you carry this lane. Hollow: measured, not carried.
Click
On a line, a dot, a row or a chip: show only that model on that harness. Inside a selection, a click on a dot shows only that effort.
Shift-click
Add or remove a model, an effort or a harness. A click on the only one, or Clear, returns the group to all. Esc clears models and efforts.
Reference
Models you do not carry: grey dots. Pin one in the Reference list, or click its dot, to draw it with a dashed line and a label and to add it under the table. They have no rank and no tier.
Frontier
The soft band and the rings: the best score at or below each cost, over every row on the board.
Zoom
Drag a box, pinch, or hold ⌘/Ctrl and scroll. Double-click or Reset returns to the fitted view.
Score range
Each cell spans the lowest to the highest score the board measured for that model across the shown efforts, on that board's own score axis, with the two end values. A dot is one effort; a filled dot is one you carry.
#, mean pct
The rank of the model's best carried lane by its mean percentile across the classification's boards that measured it (n of N). ~ means fewer than half did. Selection does not change the rank.
Tier now→here
For each carried lane: the tier it holds now and the tier this classification gives it, cutting the ranked lanes in the proportions the tiers hold now. The best lane is bold; ? means thin evidence.
Favours
The lowest effort whose mean percentile is within 5 points of the model's best effort.
