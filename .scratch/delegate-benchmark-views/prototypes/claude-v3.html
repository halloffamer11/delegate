Prototype 3 (Claude): plots first, one calm ranking per classification
  • The benchmark plots are the page. Each classification tab shows one score-against-cost plot per benchmark. Each model on a harness is one line that joins its efforts from low to max, so the shape of the line is the price of more effort.
  • Colour is the harness, shape is the model. Claude orange, Codex blue, Agy pink, Grok slate, Kiro green. Within a harness each model has its own marker shape, and its name sits at the end of its line.
  • Fill says what you run. Filled: a lane you carry. Hollow: a lane in the catalog you do not carry. Small hollow: measured, no lane at that effort. Grey dots are models you have no lane for; the dashed step is the frontier over every row.
  • One selection across the page. Select a model (chip, dot or ranking row) and it lights up in every plot with effort labels, its ranking rows stay bright, and a model card opens.
  • One consolidated ranking. A dot and whisker per carried lane: the dot is the mean percentile across the benchmarks that measured it, the ticks are each benchmark, the whisker is the spread. Rows are cut into tiers in the proportions held now, with the best and best-per-dollar lane named in each tier header.
  • Model card. The same dot and whisker for every effort the boards measured, with the effort the classification favours (lowest within 5 points of the best).
  • Less on screen. No effort stacks or matrices; rules sit in one collapsed section at the end.

Ranking

How to read this page
Line
One model on one harness, its efforts joined from low to max. A second harness for the same model shows only the efforts it has lanes for.
Marker
Filled: you carry this lane. Hollow: the lane is in the catalog, not carried. Small hollow: the board measured this effort and you have no lane for it.
Grey dot
A model you have no lane for, kept for context.
Frontier
The dashed step: the best score at or below each cost, over every row on the board.
Percentile
The share of rows on the board that the score beats (ties count half).
Classification score
The mean percentile across the classification's benchmarks that measured the lane at its effort. The ticks are each benchmark and the whisker is the spread. A hollow dot and ~ mean fewer than half the benchmarks measured it.
Tier here
The measured lanes cut in the proportions the tiers hold now; equal scores share the better tier. ▲ or ▼ is the distance from the tier the lane holds now.
Relative cost
The boards use different cost bases, so cost is the lane's cost over the board's median row cost, as a geometric mean across benchmarks.
Favoured effort
For a model, the lowest effort whose classification score is within 5 points of its best effort.
