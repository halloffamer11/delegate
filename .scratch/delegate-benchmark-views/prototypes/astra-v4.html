Round 4 · what changed
  • Plots lead: two across at most, with full-width expansion and a shared quantitative table.
  • Log cost keeps cheap efforts apart. Each board retains its original score units and cost basis.
  • Wheel and pinch anchor the zoom; drag pans. Screen-sized markers reveal space as the data spreads.
  • Labels are laid out afresh, checking every marker and prior label. Zoom reveals line names first, then efforts.
  • A five-ink palette uses blue, sky, plum, amber and slate, with light/dark variants. It avoids traffic-light semantics; model shapes, dash patterns and harness text support colour-blind reading.
  • Filled marks mean carried evidence; hollow marks mean measured only. Frontier rings indicate cost–score efficiency.
  • Table ranges span actual effort scores. Hover links views; selection persists; an optional effort filter narrows plots.
  • Ranking, coverage, tier changes and favoured efforts remain available in disclosures.

Ranking, evidence and controls

Each carried lane ranks by mean percentile across the boards with explicit evidence for that lane and effort; ties share rank. Suggested tiers split ranked lanes into quarters, 4 highest. Coverage is always n/N; missing boards are not zero. The table's plain rank describes its best carried lane; expand a model for every carried lane. Table marks are raw score ranges, never percentile ranges or uncertainty estimates.

Favoured effort: cheapest catalog effort within 3 percentile points of its model's best, using only boards measuring every candidate effort. Cost is a geometric mean of board cost / board median cost. Tier value leaders use classification score / this relative cost, an index rather than literal dollars; leaders compare the widest available coverage in each tier. Composite results are reference only.

Shared model measurements appear as context on each harness. Only explicit lane references count toward ranks and filled carried marks. Frontier rings compare catalog lane evidence at all measured efforts, not external models. AA costs are the supplied shared composite proxy; whole-run costs remain on their own board. Zero cost uses a linear axis; unknown costs remain in the table only.

Wheel or trackpad zooms about the pointer. One finger or mouse drag pans; two fingers pinch and pan. Double-click or Reset restores the full view. Focus a plot for +/− zoom, arrow-key pan, or Home to reset. Focus a mark and press Enter to select. Coincident plot points cycle on repeated clicks; every effort is also individually focusable in the table.
