Prototype 4 (Claude): zoomable plots first
  • The plots are the page. One score-against-cost plot per benchmark, two to a row, any one opens full width. Each model on a harness is one line through its efforts, low to max.
  • Zoom that clears clutter. Drag a box to zoom into it; pinch or ⌘/Ctrl-scroll zooms about the pointer; double-click resets. Markers and lines get thinner as you zoom, and effort labels appear where they fit without overlapping.
  • A designed harness palette. Codex blue, Claude orange, Kiro teal, Agy plum, Grok ink; checked for colour blindness in both themes. No red, yellow or green carries a meaning. Models inside a harness differ by marker shape.
  • Fill says what you run. Filled: a lane you carry. Hollow: measured, not carried. Grey dots: models with no lane. The soft band and rings mark the frontier.
  • Effort range table. One row per model on a harness; each benchmark cell is the real score range across the efforts measured, one dot per effort, filled where you carry it. No percentiles.
  • Linked. Select a model in a chip, plot or row: it stays bright everywhere else and its efforts get labels.

How to read this page
Line
One model on one harness, its measured efforts joined from low to max. A second harness for the same model draws only the efforts it has lanes for.
Marker
Colour is the harness and shape is the model. Filled: you carry this lane. Hollow: measured, not carried.
Grey dot
A model with no lane, kept for context. Hide them with the "Other models" switch.
Frontier
The soft band and the rings: the best score at or below each cost, over every row on the board.
Zoom
Drag a box, or pinch, or hold ⌘/Ctrl and scroll. Double-click or Reset returns to the full view.
Effort range
Each cell spans the lowest to the highest score the board measured for that model across its efforts, on that board's own score axis. A dot is one effort; a filled dot is one you carry.
